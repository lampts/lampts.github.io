How banks evaluate AI voice agents in production

Voice agents have to be evaluated in production, because a demo cannot show how an agent handles noisy lines or rare questions. Lam Pham's team runs 150+ voice agents at Cake and is writing their evaluation playbook around three checks:

  • Signal-to-noise monitoring tests whether the agent still understands the caller when line quality drops.
  • Golden QA sets are fixed questions with known correct answers, used to check what the agents say.
  • Response-mode systems set how an agent responds in each type of situation.

In 2025, Cake's voice agents were reported to raise customer reach 80% and debt recovery 30%, and to cut operating cost 40%.

How AI is used in credit decisions at Cake

Cake uses AI models to decide who gets credit and to forecast revenue. Lam Pham's team builds those models and teaches the team causal inference on real credit cases, because a model can predict who defaults without showing what a policy change would cause. The same 2025 Vietnam Banks Association article reports that AI credit scoring at Cake raised approvals 20% and cut bad debt 40%.

How LLMs cut the cost of analyzing Vietnamese financial reports

Lam Pham's intel-brief pipeline cut the cost of reading a Vietnamese financial report from $8 to $0.80. It converts each report from PDF to markdown to SQL, so later questions query tables instead of rereading pages.

Engineering principles

Lam Pham works by five principles:

Unix
Small composable tools that do one thing.
Slope over intercept
Hiring and building for learning rate rather than current knowledge.
Farmer
Improving conditions without forcing growth.
Hard to vary
Removing any part of an explanation or system that is only decorative.
Ship over paper
A model in production beats a model in a notebook.
