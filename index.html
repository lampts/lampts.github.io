How I Build
Unix
Small composable tools that multiply force. One tool does one thing. Pipe them for complex behavior.
Slope > Intercept
Hire for learning rate, not current knowledge. Build for compounding. Applies to people, models, and systems.
Farmer
Optimize conditions, nurture without forcing, accept seasonal variability. Patient cultivation beats forced growth.
Hard to Vary
Good explanations have parts you can't swap without breaking the whole. If an element is decorative, remove it.
Ship > Paper
A model in production beats a model in a notebook. The march of nines starts after the demo works.
