What’s next

Moves sized on October 2026. Each number is computed from the pipeline; the recommendation is the analyst’s. Evidence ↗

01

Patch on exploitation, not on CVSS

8,570 October 2026 CVEs are high or critical. Only 32 of them are in KEV or have EPSS ≥ 10% today. A fast lane for those shrinks the urgent queue by 99.6%; re-run monthly, since KEV and EPSS catch up on new CVEs.

02

Stop waiting for NVD

71% of the month is unanalysed by NVD; for 11,159 of those 12,039 the CNA's own CVSS is already there. Take CNA scores and CISA SSVC (94% coverage) as the first triage signal.

03

Lock down agent and LLM-app builders

14 AI-stack CVEs joined KEV in 2026, mostly LiteLLM, Langflow, MLflow, Ray. Inventory these tools, take them off the internet, and patch them on the KEV clock.

04

Screen dependencies against the malicious-package feed

107 AI-themed malicious PyPI packages in 2026, and real packages (litellm, mistralai) were hijacked. Block the OSV malicious list at the package mirror and check lockfiles.

05

Read the tier-1 press as a priority signal

Tier-1 outlets named 62 CVEs in the last 30 days; 33 of them (53%) are in KEV, against 0.14% of all CVEs published in October 2026.

06

Monitor advisories and research, not just CVEs

In 2026: 233 prompt-injection and 954 MCP advisories; 85 exist only as GitHub advisories with no CVE, and NVD tags none of them CWE-1427. Pull GHSA and the AI-security research feeds alongside NVD: they carried 15 researcher and vendor posts on these themes and on attackers using AI in the last 30 days.

Appendix

DefinitionsEvery KPI in one line
  • CVEs published: CVE records with an NVD publication date in the month, rejected IDs excluded.
  • Critical / high: Best available CVSS per CVE (v4.0 > v3.1 > v3.0 > v2; NVD primary before CNA secondary), banded on the v3 scale: critical ≥ 9, high ≥ 7.
  • KEV adds: CVEs CISA added to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue in the month (dateAdded), any CVE year.
  • EPSS ≥ 10%: CVEs published in the month whose current FIRST EPSS score is at least 0.10.
  • Not analysed by NVD: Status today is Received, Awaiting Analysis, Undergoing Analysis or Deferred.
  • Top-3 CNA share: CVEs filed by the month's three busiest CVE Numbering Authorities ÷ the month's CVEs.
  • AI-stack CVE: Matched to one of about 60 AI / data-science components by OSV PyPI package, NVD vendor/product, or (apps only) description.
  • Prompt-injection / MCP / agent-abuse advisory: A CVE or GitHub advisory whose text matches the theme rules in llmsec.THEMES (CVEs on MCP components also count as MCP), deduplicated by CVE ID. 'No CVE' = GHSA advisory without one.
  • AI-themed malicious package: OSV MAL-* PyPI record that is a same-name hijack, separator trick, typo (Damerau-Levenshtein ≤ 1, ≤ 2 for long names) or combosquat of a popular AI package, or carries an AI brand.
MethodWindows, sources and caveats
  • Headline month: October 2026; November 2026 is in progress and skipped.
  • YoY: Same calendar month a year earlier; rates compare in percentage points.
  • Sources: NVD 2.0 via the fkie-cad daily mirror; CISA KEV; FIRST EPSS; NVD source API for CNA names; OSV.dev PyPI dump; RSS from Calif, Google Threat Intelligence, Google Project Zero, CISA Advisories, Cisco Talos, watchTowr Labs, Simon Willison (prompt injection), Embrace The Red, Trail of Bits, OWASP GenAI Security Project, Microsoft Security Blog, Unit 42, Socket, arXiv (prompt injection, MCP).
  • Recency bias: Status, EPSS and KEV are read today, so recent months look less analysed and less exploited than they will.
