Strategy Guide

Villain Types

TAG (Tight-Aggressive) 🦈

Plays few hands but aggressively. VPIP 15-22%, PFR 12-20%.

Exploit: Steal blinds, fold to their aggression.

LAG (Loose-Aggressive) 🔥

Plays many hands aggressively. VPIP 30-50%, PFR 25-40%.

Exploit: Let them bluff, call down light, trap monsters.

LAP (Loose-Passive) 🎰

Plays many hands but rarely raises. VPIP 35-55%, PFR 5-15%.

Exploit: Value bet relentlessly, NEVER bluff.

TAP (Tight-Passive) 🐢

Plays few hands and rarely raises. VPIP 10-18%, PFR 5-12%.

Exploit: Bluff relentlessly, fold when they bet.

📖 Glossary
TAG 🦈
Tight-Aggressive. Few hands, bets them hard. The competent player. Plays ~18% of hands.
LAG 🔥
Loose-Aggressive. Many hands, bets them hard. The maniac. Plays ~38% of hands, bluffs frequently.
LAP 🎰
Loose-Passive. Many hands, rarely raises. The calling station. Your ATM.
TAP 🐢
Tight-Passive. Few hands, rarely raises. The nit. When they bet, they have it.
One-Line Exploits
TAGSteal blinds, fold to their aggression
LAGCall down light, trap monsters, no bluffs
LAPValue bet relentlessly, NEVER bluff
TAPBluff relentlessly, fold when they bet
VPIP
Voluntarily Put $ In Pot. % of hands played.
• <20%: Tight | 20-35%: Standard | >35%: Loose
PFR
Preflop Raise %.
• <15%: Passive | 15-25%: Standard | >25%: Aggressive
AF
Aggression Factor. (Bets + Raises) / Calls
• <1: Passive | 1-2: Balanced | >2: Aggressive | >4: Maniac
WTSD
Went To Showdown %.
• <25%: Folds a lot | 25-35%: Standard | >35%: Calling station
BTN Button
Best position. Acts last postflop. Information advantage.
BB Big Blind
Worst position heads-up. Acts first postflop.
IP / OOP
In Position / Out of Position. IP = acting last (advantage). OOP = acting first (disadvantage).
M-Ratio
M = Stack / (SB + BB)
How many orbits you can survive.
MZoneStrategy
>20GREENFull strategy
10-20YELLOWTighten ranges
6-10ORANGEPush/fold approaching

EV (Expected Value)
Average chips won/lost over infinite repetitions.
• +EV = profitable | -EV = losing
EV Formulas
EV(Fold) = 0
EV(Call) = Equity × Pot - Call
EV(Raise) = FE × Pot + (1-FE) × (Eq × NewPot - Raise)
Pot Odds
Call / (Pot + Call). If Equity > Pot Odds → +EV call.
Fold Equity
Probability villain folds.
LAP: ~30% | LAG: ~35% | TAG: ~50% | TAP: ~70%
Rule of 2/4
Flop: Outs × 4 = % to hit by river
Turn: Outs × 2 = % to hit river
Value Bet
Bet when worse hands call.
Bluff
Bet when better hands fold.
Semi-Bluff
Bluff with a draw. Win now or hit later.
C-bet
Continuation bet. Bet flop after raising preflop.
Pot Control
Keep pot small with medium hands.
Trap
Check strong hands to let villain bluff.
Float
Call flop IP to take pot later.
Blocker
Card reducing villain's possible hands.
