How This Simulation Works

This tool runs a Monte Carlo simulation to forecast the total month's revenue. It simulates the remaining days of the month thousands of times to build a range of possible outcomes.

1. Baseline & Volatility
  • Baseline (Current Avg): The simulation's starting point is the average daily revenue you've achieved so far this month (meanNov14).
  • Volatility (Std Dev): It models "normal" randomness using a weighted average of this month's volatility (stdNov14) and the volatility from the second half of October (stdOct15p).
2. Daily Simulation Logic

For each simulation, it "lives out" the rest of the month, one day at a time. Each simulated day's revenue is calculated by combining four factors:

  • Downtrend: A slight negative trend (-0.625/day, learned from Previous month) is applied, modeling a natural sales decline post-mid-month.
  • Seasonal Event: On event days (e.g., Black Friday), the revenue is multiplied by a random boost (e.g., +5% to +15%).
  • Downtime Risk: Each day has a small chance of a "downtime event" (based on Expected Downtime Events/Month). If it happens, that day's revenue is reduced by the Downtime Impact %.
  • Random Noise: Finally, it adds the "normal" volatility (from Step 1) to make the day unique.
3. Interpreting the Output
  • Success Rate: The percentage of all simulations that met or exceeded the Target Revenue. This is the "probability of success."
  • Expected Total: The *average* total revenue across all simulations. This is the most statistically likely outcome.
  • Gap vs Required: This KPI shows if your Current Avg is ahead (+) or behind (-) the *daily average* you need for the rest of the month to hit your target.
  • Forecast Zone Chart: Shows daily variance (Left Y-Axis) and the P10-P90 cumulative path to target (Right Y-Axis).
