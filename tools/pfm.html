Module 1 / 7

The Fundamental Equation of Wealth

"All of personal finance is just this equation, iterated."

Most people think about money wrong. They focus on:

❌ "How much do I make?" (income)
❌ "How much did I spend?" (expenses)

The correct question:

✅ "What is my NET WORTH and how is it changing?"

The Fundamental Equation
Wealth(t+1) = Wealth(t) + Income(t) - Expenses(t) + Returns(t)

This is the ONLY equation you need. Everything else is optimization.

Stocks vs Flows

STOCKS (point in time): Net worth, Debt, Assets

FLOWS (over a period): Income, Expenses, Savings rate

The Credit Card Float

"Use other people's money for free, legally."

When you pay with a credit card:

  1. You spend money you don't have (yet)
  2. The bank pays the merchant
  3. You have up to 45 days before paying the bank
  4. Your cash earns interest during those 45 days

This is called the FLOAT.

Module 4 / 7

Dollar Cost Averaging

"Time in market beats timing the market."

DCA = Invest fixed amount at fixed intervals, regardless of price.

  • When prices are LOW, you buy MORE shares
  • When prices are HIGH, you buy FEWER shares
  • Result: Average cost < Average price

When DCA Wins

DCA wins in: Bear markets, Volatile markets. Lump Sum wins in: Steady bull markets. Since we CAN'T PREDICT markets, DCA is the safer default.

    "Your 4-asset portfolio is really X independent bets."

    "PCA always gives you something. But if eigenvalues are similar, you're finding noise, not structure."

    Rule: Each PC should explain 10× more variance than the next. If not, your "diversification" is an illusion.
