Community & Product

"The best marketing doesn't feel like marketing."

❌ Wrong Approach

Treat community as a broadcast channel. Spam Discord with product updates. Gate all value behind paywalls.

✅ Right Approach

Make the product so good people can't stop sharing. Community becomes a flywheel, not a megaphone.

THE FREE TIER IS YOUR CAC. CALCULATE IT OR DIE.

Module 3 / 7

Paid Acquisition

"Paid channels are a tax on not having product-market fit."

❌ Wrong Approach

Scale spend before understanding unit economics. Target broad audiences. Optimize for impressions.

✅ Right Approach

Know your CAC:LTV before scaling. Obsessively narrow targeting. Optimize for conversions.

2 Channels
SEM (Search) — Captures existing intent
Example: Writesonic bidding on "AI content generator"
Social/Display Ads — Creates new demand
Example: Copy.ai targeting writers on LinkedIn
CAC:LTV Viability Check CHALLENGE

Rule of thumb: LTV should be 3x+ CAC for paid to work long-term.

SEM = CAPTURE DEMAND. SOCIAL = CREATE DEMAND. CHOOSE YOUR BATTLE.

Module 4 / 7

Targeted Acquisition

"The founder closes the first 10 deals. Then you hire sales."

❌ Wrong Approach

Hire sales before founder-led selling. Send generic blast emails. Skip personalization.

✅ Right Approach

Close 10-15 deals yourself first. Send behavior-triggered, personalized emails. Research before outreach.

3 Channels
Sales — Glean (high ACV enterprise)
Email Marketing — Rewind AI (waitlist nurturing)
Targeting Blogs — beehiiv (niche creator outreach)
Sales vs PLG Decision Matrix CHALLENGE

High ACV + complex sale = sales team. Low ACV + simple = PLG. Where do you fit?

FOUNDER-LED SALES FIRST. ALWAYS. NO EXCEPTIONS.

Module 6 / 7

Physical & High-Touch

"A booth without booked meetings is just expensive decoration."

❌ Wrong Approach

Scan badges and call it success. Host events without clear goals. Run offline ads with no CTA.

✅ Right Approach

Book qualified meetings for next week. Define event goals (recruiting? leads? feedback?). Measurable CTAs only.

Offline Ads — Scale AI (SFO airport billboards)

Is that $20K booth worth it? Do the math.

TRADE SHOWS ARE FOR BOOKING MEETINGS. EVERYTHING ELSE IS NOISE.

Q1 — Community & Product Unanswered

In a PLG model, what is the "free tier" effectively acting as?

Correct! The free tier cost = your CAC. If 10,000 free users cost $5,000/mo to serve and convert 300 to paid, your CAC is $16.67.
Q2 — Content & Brand Unanswered

Which keyword type should you prioritize for content that converts?

Correct! Intent > Volume. 100 buyers searching "best AI writing tool" beat 10,000 browsers searching "what is AI."
Q3 — Paid Acquisition Unanswered

What's the minimum LTV:CAC ratio for sustainable paid acquisition?

Correct! 3:1 is the rule of thumb. Below that, you're likely not accounting for overhead, churn, and scaling costs.
Q4 — Targeted Acquisition Unanswered

When should a startup hire their first salesperson?

Correct! Founder-led sales first. Always. You can't train salespeople on a playbook you haven't written yourself.
Q5 — Ecosystem & Partnerships Unanswered

What's the right approach to working with influencers/creators?

Correct! Dictating creative backfires. Influencers know their audience—trust them, or the partnership will feel forced and fail.
Q6 — Physical & High-Touch Unanswered

What's the primary goal of a trade show booth?

Correct! Trade shows are for booking meetings. Badge scans are vanity metrics. Meetings that convert to pipeline are the goal.
Q7 — Bullseye Framework Unanswered

What does "Product-Channel Fit" mean?

Correct! Product-Channel Fit > Product-Market Fit. Find the ONE channel where your product spreads, then saturate it before moving on.
